# ASILI Engineering Solutions: Brand Concept

Direction: **Rooted Power** (grounded, dependable, local)

## Mission
To give farms, homes and businesses power they can rely on, by sizing every system correctly and testing and commissioning every circuit before we hand it over.

## Primary audience
A farmer who needs a solar system designed and installed to run an irrigation pump. He wants water on his crops every day, and he wants to deal with an engineer who explains things plainly.

Also served: homeowners and commercial or industrial clients who need electrical installations, and people who need a website or mobile app.

## Positioning against competitors
Most customers can choose a cheaper installer. The usual result is wrongly sized components, and circuits that are unsafe, unprotected and never tested.

ASILI is the engineering company that works out the right size by calculation, protects every circuit, and hands over only after testing and commissioning. Others sell an installation. ASILI sells a system that works and is safe.

## Three brand pillars
1. **Sized right.** Every system is calculated for the real load, whether that is a pump, a home or a factory. Nothing is guessed.
2. **Tested before handover.** Every circuit is protected, tested and commissioned before the customer pays the final bill.
3. **Rooted here.** "Asili" means roots or origin in Swahili. ASILI is local, speaks plainly, and is one call or WhatsApp message away.

## Voice and tone
ASILI sounds like a qualified engineer talking to a neighbour: plain, direct and warm. Sentences are short. Technical terms are explained or left out. Every claim is about what the customer gets (water, light, machines that keep running), not about how clever the engineering is.

**Five words we always use**
- sized
- protected
- tested
- commissioned
- reliable

**Five words we never use**
- cheap (ASILI competes on doing it properly, not on price)
- roughly (the whole promise is that nothing is guessed)
- quick fix
- world-class
- cutting-edge

## Visual feel
The brand looks like a well-kept farm at sunrise: deep forest green, warm sunlight and clean open space. Large blocks of forest green carry the headlines in white. Sun yellow is saved for the one main button on each screen, so the eye always finds "WhatsApp us" or "Send an enquiry" first. Pages sit on a pale field green with plain white cards and gently rounded corners. Headlines are set in Barlow Semi Condensed Bold, which is modelled on road signs and equipment labels and so reads as practical engineering. Body text is Barlow, at a comfortable size for reading on a phone outdoors. Photos are ASILI's own finished jobs in daylight, never stock images. The logo always sits on a light background, because its greens disappear against the forest green.
